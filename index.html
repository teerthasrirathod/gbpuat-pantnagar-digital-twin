Data sources

    Official figures (gbpuat.ac.in): campus 16,000 acres (about 65 km²), of which the University Farm is nearly 6,000 acres (about 24 km²). The yellow outlines show only the parts mapped in OSM. Building heights are estimated (from tags, or from footprint size for detected buildings). Detected footprints are machine-generated from satellite images and can be wrong. The official campus boundary is not published as GIS data, so the model covers the whole study window. Not survey-grade; for visualisation and planning discussions only.
