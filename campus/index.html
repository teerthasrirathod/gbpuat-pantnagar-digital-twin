        How to move

        AerialDrag to orbit, right-drag or Shift-drag to pan, wheel to zoom, double-click to fly there. Click a building or a block for details.
        Street viewPress Street view, then click a road. ↑/W forward, ↓/S back, drag to look around. At junctions click an arrow or use ←/→ then ↑.
        Free walkW A S D or arrows to walk anywhere, drag to look, Shift to run. Buildings block you.
        PlanPress Plan (or P), pick a tool and click on the map. Buildings and areas: three clicks for a rectangle. Roads: click along the route, double-click or Enter to finish. Esc stops a tool, Delete removes the selected item, Ctrl+Z undoes. The Simulate tab shows Wi-Fi, lighting and walking access; Proposal makes a report.
        KeysV street view, P plan, Esc back to aerial, / search, R reset view, ? this help.

        Buildings: OpenStreetMap, Google and Microsoft footprints, and extra buildings found in Google's 2023 building-presence raster. Heights are measured where Google's 2.5D data covers them. Facades, windows and roof details are generated from height, floors and type, not photographed.
