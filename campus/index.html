        How to move

        AerialFly with W A S D or the arrow keys (hold Shift for speed), turn with Q / E, zoom with + / - or the wheel. With the mouse: drag to orbit, right-drag or Shift-drag to pan, double-click to fly there. Click a building or a block for details.
        Street viewPress Street view (or V), then click anywhere on the ground. You roam freely: W/↑ forward, S/↓ back, A D sidestep, ← → or Q E turn, drag to look around, Shift to run. Buildings block you. Follow road keeps you on the road network instead, with arrows at junctions.
        Try an ideaPress Try an idea (or P) and follow the four steps: Goal, Changes, Results, Share. To place something, choose it and click on the map, or press Enter or Space to put it at the cross in the middle of the screen. Drag to move the map, right-drag to turn it. Esc stops, Delete removes the selected item, Ctrl+Z undoes.
        KeysV street view, P plan, Esc back to aerial (while planning it stops what you are doing), / search, R reset view, ? this help. If letter keys get in your way, switch them off under Display while planning.

        Buildings: OpenStreetMap, Google and Microsoft footprints, and extra buildings found in Google's 2023 building-presence raster. Heights are measured where Google's 2.5D data covers them. Facades, windows and roof details are generated from height, floors and type, not photographed.
